🛠️ SANCTUM MANUAL

SomniaSanctum is your offline, local-first somatic vault. It tracks the body without judgment, utilizing tap-based inputs to reduce cognitive load.

  • Global Snapshot Tool: Located in the top navigation bar. Clicking 📸 Snapshot will evaluate your current somatic logs, prompt the Alchemist to generate and log insights based on your recent patterns, and trigger a raw JSON download of your biological data to your hard drive.
  • Strict Separation of Data: The Vault Tab purely imports/exports aesthetic Vibes (Colors, Particles, Audio layouts). You must use the 📸 Snapshot tool to export your actual somatic logs.
  • Daily Check-in: Designed for ND-friendly friction. Only tap what you want. Data merges automatically under the current local date.
  • Calendar Colors:
    Good (Mood 4-5) |
    Meh (Mood 3) |
    Rough (Mood 1-2).

🤖 THE ALCHEMIST (AI)

Enter your Gemini API key in The Alchemist tab. Your key stays localized. Ask the Alchemist to cross-reference your symptoms or provide a weekly summary based on the raw data in your Sanctum.
